The New Jersey Department of Education (revised January 2026) issued guidance requiring all public school boards to adopt a bell-to-bell policy prohibiting student use of personal internet-enabled devices during the school day. Smart glasses are explicitly listed among the prohibited device types alongside smartphones, tablets, and smartwatches.
