DEF CON 2026 has banned camera-equipped smart glasses with no exemption for prescription lenses, joining a wider set of organisers (Monopoly Events, CalMac) restricting such devices amid mounting privacy concerns around Meta's Ray-Ban/Oakley wearables, including covert recording, doxxing potential, and UK ICO scrutiny over footage reviewed by contractors in Kenya.
