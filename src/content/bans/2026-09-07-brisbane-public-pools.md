Brisbane City Council banned smart glasses at public pools — the first such local ban in Australia — prohibiting non-consensual recording and giving lifeguards powers to evict violators. Experts note enforcement is undermined by the difficulty of visually identifying the devices in use, and that meaningful regulation requires state or federal action.
