Scotland's courts have banned Meta smart glasses from being used to record in court and tribunal buildings, mirroring a confiscation policy adopted in England and Wales and adding to a growing list of venues — CalMac ferries, restaurants, pubs, theatres — that restrict the devices over privacy concerns.
