Caledonian MacBrayne (CalMac) paused public bridge visits on its vessels after a passenger secretly filmed in the restricted bridge area using Meta smart glasses without permission. CalMac said: "We have temporarily paused bridge visits on vessels by members of the public after an incident where an individual filmed in a restricted area without permission," adding that public safety and well-being remain the priority.
